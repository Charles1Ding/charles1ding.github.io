U-235 Nuclear Fission

How ²³⁵U splits into fission fragments — and how the proton number (Z) changes yet stays conserved.

²³⁵U · Z = 92

One fission event yields two fragments + ~2–3 neutrons. I-131, Cs-137 and ⁹⁰Sr are three different fission channels, each with its real complementary fragment. Hit “Fire all 3” to watch all three channels split at once. Proton total is always conserved: 92 = Z₁ + Z₂; emitted neutrons carry Z = 0.
