Heat the sugar on the pudding. Two things break — and they are not the same thing.

Solid sugar. The molecules are locked in a lattice.
Legend
Melting — physical change. The dashed links give way. The molecules drift apart and the sugar flows, but every molecule is still intact: it is still sugar.

Caramelisation — chemical change. Now the solid bonds inside each molecule break. The molecules fall apart, water is driven off, and new brown compounds form. It is no longer sugar.
